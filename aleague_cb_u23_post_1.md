# Centre-Backs in the A-League to Look Out For (Under 23)

Australian football rarely gets the spotlight it deserves, and that's especially true at centre-back — a position where patience and physical development often matter more than early hype. This week we're looking at three under-23 centre-backs quietly putting together the kind of season-long numbers that suggest they won't be playing at this level for long.

---

## Sebastian Esposito (Melbourne Victory, 20)

Esposito's path already sets him apart: developed at Melbourne City, he made the move to Serie A side Lecce in January 2024 before returning to Australia on loan with Melbourne Victory to get regular senior minutes. He's since become a fixture in Victory's backline and captained Australia's U20s to their first-ever AFC U20 Asian Cup title.

**Season so far:** 24 matches, 1,883 minutes, 29 tackles won, 24 interceptions.

Esposito's numbers reflect a defender trusted with a genuinely full-time role rather than rotation minutes — and with a parent club in Serie A still holding his contract, he's arguably the closest thing on this list to a player already being watched by Europe.

## Panagiotis Kikianis (Adelaide United, 20)

A product of Adelaide United's own academy, Kikianis signed his first professional deal back in September 2023 and has developed into one of the more well-rounded young centre-backs in the competition — comfortable enough on the ball to have already picked up 2 goals this season, including a dramatic 98th-minute equaliser as an 18-year-old. He's a current Australia U20 international.

**Season so far:** 23 matches, 2,042 minutes, 27 tackles won, 23 interceptions.

Few defenders his age in the league are logging this volume of both minutes and defensive actions — a strong signal of a manager's trust.

## Isaac Hughes (Wellington Phoenix, 21)

English-born but a New Zealand youth international (U20 and U23), Hughes moved to New Zealand at age 9 and worked his way up through the Phoenix academy, captaining their reserves before graduating to the first team in 2023. Coaches have described him as a "combative centre back" — and the numbers back that up.

**Season so far:** 23 matches, 2,005 minutes, 19 tackles won, 24 interceptions.

Hughes has quietly become one of the most consistent starters in this group, ever-present in Wellington's defence this season.

---

### A note on the numbers

Stats referenced here (tackles won, interceptions) are drawn from FBref's basic season data. Deeper metrics like clearances and progressive passing — which would normally round out a defensive profile — currently aren't available following FBref's loss of its Opta data licence in January 2026, which affected advanced stats site-wide. As more data sources become available, future updates to this list will include a fuller statistical picture.
